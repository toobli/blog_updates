Identity · naming

Always written as ShieldTX

The brand name is one word: capital S, capital T, capital X. No space, no hyphen, no lowercase x. Use the same form in body copy, headlines, code, and product UI.

Do
  • ✓ ShieldTX

One word. Capital S, T, X. Lowercase 'hield'.

Don't
  • ✕ Shield TX (no space)
  • ✕ ShieldTx (lowercase x)
  • ✕ Shield-TX (no hyphen)
  • ✕ shieldtx (no all-lowercase)
  • ✕ SHIELDTX (no all-caps in prose)
  • ✕ Shield Tx (no title-case)
Identity · TX treatment

One mark, two TX treatments

The crossed-line decoration on the TX is a feature of the standalone logotype only. In the lockup (mark + logotype), TX is rendered as flat letterforms so the symbol carries the visual weight.

Do

Lockup — TX rendered as flat letterforms. Use anywhere the mark accompanies the wordmark (nav, footer, app chrome).

Logotype only

Standalone logotype — the crossed-line TX appears only when the logotype runs without the mark (long marketing displays, signature blocks, brand-led visuals).
